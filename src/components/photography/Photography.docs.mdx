# WMATA Photography

WMATA photography uses real people, real movement, and real Metro experiences.

The photography communicates:

- **Authenticity** – Real riders and staff  
- **Energy** – Motion, commuting, transit activity  
- **Warmth** – Soft shadows, warm color grading  
- **Clarity** – Clean subject focus  

---

## Categories

| Category | Usage |
|---------|-------|
| **Candid** | Real-life moments with natural expressions |
| **Lifestyle** | Emotional storytelling and context-rich scenes |
| **Transportation** | Metro buses, trains, and mobility experiences |
